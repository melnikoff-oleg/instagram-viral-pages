# concept

`instagram-concept.md` is the source of truth for the page: what it is, who it is for, how it looks, how a series is built, what the prompts must obey. It does not exist until step 1 writes it; the studio's Concept card stays empty until then.

`examples/iconic-cars-x-iconic-places.md` is the concept this system was built on (iconic cars in iconic places, shot like stills from a film that was never made), and the shape to copy: the idea in one line, what the account is, three pillars, the look in detail, the series structure, the subject and place pools, prompting rules learned the hard way, the tone. `examples/iconic-cars-x-iconic-places-ideas.md` is the raw idea list it came from.

`ctlt-image-prompt-template.md` is the four-paragraph shape of every image prompt: Camera → Tone → Lighting → Texture.

A good concept passes three tests: the subject and the place are recognised in under a second; it repeats as a series without getting boring; it has one look you can describe in five lines.
