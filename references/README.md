# references

`visual-style/` holds the style references every image prompt points at (style only: grain, colour, light, motion blur; never the subjects, and never posted). The default set is the one this system was built on; drop your own stills onto the References card in the studio to replace or extend it, then press "Analyse my references" so this file describes the new look.

`headshot.*` is the person's face, the identity reference for the profile picture. Drop it onto the References card.

## The default look, in ten lines

1. Vertical 9:16, shot from far away on a long lens, so the subject sits small against the place.
2. A smooth, cloudless twilight sky, cool at the top fading to dusty rose near the horizon, 50–70% of the frame.
3. A low horizon; the ground dark, warm brown to near-black, soft or streaked with motion.
4. The subject reads as a dark silhouette: exposed for the sky, crushed warm blacks.
5. One hot warm light that belongs to the subject (headlights, flames, sparks), slightly clipped, blooming, casting a small glow on the ground beneath it.
6. Split toning: cool highlights, warm orange light, brown mids and shadows; low saturation everywhere else.
7. Coarse film grain with coloured speckle, most visible in the sky.
8. Nothing tack sharp: panning streaks, haze, dust or spray near the lens.
9. Something near the lens and something near the subject in every frame, so the clip has layers that move.
10. No people, no text, no visible sun. Like an unpublished set still from a film that was never made.
