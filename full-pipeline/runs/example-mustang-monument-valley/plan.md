# Run: 1967 Ford Mustang Fastback × Monument Valley

2026-10-29, third run of `full-pipeline/`, the first on the 4 × 5 s structure. Earlier series: Murciélago × Giza, G-Wagon × Red Square, F40 × Colosseum (kept, not posted), GT-R × Fuji.

## Why this pairing

- American icon meets American icon. The '67 fastback's long hood, sloping roofline and three-bar taillights read even blurred; the Mittens and Merrick Butte are the Forrest Gump view, recognisable from anywhere in under a second.
- Colour rule: a dark Highland Green (near-black) car against a peach twilight sky and red rock. Nothing merges.
- New emitter for the page: red dust. A rooster tail off the shoulder, a wall of it off the rear tyres in the drift, a dust storm at the peak, and the car's own cloud swallowing it at the end. After sand (Giza), snow (Red Square), rain (Colosseum) and petals (Fuji).

## Rules this run is built on

- `motion-study/sop.md`: the car is S1 (straight and fast) or S2 (drift) in every frame, never standing. One camera behaviour per clip, constant angle on the car. No jumps, closest pass 2 m, at least one emitter per clip, dust in every clip.
- Real-time speed with a number in every GOAL, "No slow motion, no gentle movement" in every EXCLUSIONS.
- First SHOT line: the first frame is exactly the picked still, the car readable, then the beat timing in seconds. The camera never names equipment and never asks for a tilting horizon.
- Opener generated alone first, frame 0 checked, before the rest.
- No Anthropic API: Pinterest screened by eye (`--screen 60`, picks #46 and #45, same car), music searches written by hand, picks judged in the session.

## Screen direction

The reference's nose points right, so the car travels **left → right** in every frame. The drift (frame 2) enters from the left and sweeps toward the lens and out right. The oncoming peak (frame 3) is direction-neutral. The ending slides left → right.

## The four frames

| # | Role | Shot (sop.md §4) | Light / weather | Frame |
|---|------|------------------|-----------------|-------|
| 1 | Opener (energy 5) | **A side track**, C2 matched track, 135mm from across the highway | Civil twilight, cloudless lavender-to-peach sky, light wind, dust in the air | Low side profile of the fastback at 100 mph on the Monument Valley straight travelling left to right, headlights on, the Mittens and Merrick Butte filling the top half behind it, a red-dust rooster tail |
| 2 | Play (energy 4) | **F drift arc past a low camera**, C1 locked with a late pan, 28mm on the ground | Late dusk, last pink, wind rising | The car mid-drift on the open red dirt below the buttes, sliding left → right toward the lens, tail out, front wheels counter-steered, a wall of red dust off the rear tyres, body leaning on the outside suspension |
| 3 | Peak (energy 5) | **C oncoming grow**, C1 locked, 35mm beside the car's line | Blue hour, a dust storm rolling across the valley, the buttes half-veiled | The car punching out of the dust wall straight at the camera, headlights blazing through the dust, the buttes looming above |
| 4 | Funny ending (energy 3) | **Slide to stop**, C1 locked, 50mm low beside the road | Night, a huge full moon between the buttes, the storm passing, dust still in the air | The car skidding to a stop side-on in front of the buttes, its own giant dust cloud still travelling behind it; in the video it rolls over the car and swallows it, two headlights glowing out of the cloud |

Energy curve 5-4-5-3. Shot codes A → F → C → stop: none repeated in a row, the backdrop changes in every frame (buttes behind a side profile, buttes above a drift on dirt, buttes above the dust wall, buttes with the moon).

## Video beats (5 s each)

1. Opener, A: the camera travels alongside at almost the car's speed, a touch slower, so the car gains from the left third toward the centre while the buttes and the fence posts streak behind it; the rooster tail hangs across the frame behind the car.
2. Play, F: the car is already mid-drift in the first frame, sweeps the arc toward the lens, passes 3 m away throwing red dust at the lens for a few frames, the camera pans a beat late to follow it out right, dust rolling across the dirt after it.
3. Peak, C: the car grows out of the dust wall from mid-frame to nearly full frame in about two seconds, tears past 2 m to the right of the lens, the camera flinches a beat late, the dust wall rolls over the camera behind it.
4. Ending: the car slides left → right under braking, rocks to a stop in the centre of the frame, and its own dust cloud catches up and swallows it completely; two headlight glows out of the red cloud, the dust still moving on the last frame.
