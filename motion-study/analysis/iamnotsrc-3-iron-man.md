## What the video is doing

- **Three clips of 5 s, one idea each.** Fly past, fly with, land. Each clip is a single verb.
- **The subject is pinned, the world moves.** In 1 and 2 Iron Man stays in roughly the same part of the frame; the camera is a pan or a matched track and the background does the work.
- **The only deceleration is the ending.** The landing is the "stop" beat, and it is still a motion (descent + flames + dust) until the final second.
- **Realism:** sun flare from behind, lens-dirty highlights, long-lens compression on the city, afterburner glow that lights the fuselages, grain. The flat pink sky in clip 2 is the least real moment: no parallax, so the eye has nothing to judge speed by except the exhaust.
- **Hook:** frame 0 already has the subject at speed, thrusters lit, readable silhouette on a sunset.

## Lessons for cars

- Pan-with-subject from a high fixed point (clip 1) is a clean car move: the car crosses, the camera pans, the landmark slides through behind. Easy to describe, very readable.
- Avoid clip 2's problem: a featureless background kills the speed read. Always give the camera something to streak: road markings, lamp posts, fences, dust, buildings, the landmark.
- A "landing" ending for a car = a slide to a stop with dust rolling past the car, not a parked car.
