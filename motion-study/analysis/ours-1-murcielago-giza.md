## What works

- Every clip has the car moving. No static car anywhere. ✔
- One vector per clip: across, toward, away, over. ✔
- The sandstorm (3) and the sand explosion (2, 4) are our "emitters": environment motion that keeps the frame alive and hides detail.
- Screen direction holds: left→right in 1, toward in 2, away in 3, left→right in 4.
- First frame: car and pyramids readable, car already rolling. ✔
- Ending: the dust swallows the car. A clean, funny-ish "gone" beat.

## What does not work (compared with the five references)

- **Clip 2 loses the subject.** From 4.0 to 5.5 s the frame is pure sand and orange paint; the viewer cannot tell what is happening for 1.5 s. The references never let the subject become unreadable for more than a few frames (Rocket's whip pan is ~0.7 s).
- **Clip 4 is physically wrong.** A Murciélago hanging in the air for two seconds over a dune reads as a game, not a film. The references keep every subject under believable physics: bikes stay on the road, jets fly, a man lands with dust. **Jumps are off the menu.**
- **Speed is uneven.** Clip 1 is a little slower than the Ghost Rider side-track; the wheels blur but the pyramids barely slide. The references keep the background smeared for the whole duration of a speed shot.
- **Too many ideas in 15 s.** Four clips, four concepts (profile, sand explosion, storm, jump). Ghost Rider used two concepts and got 4M plays. Silver Surfer used two. Each clip here is shorter than theirs, so none of them gets to breathe.
- **The camera is close and low in clip 2** with the car crossing inside a metre of the lens: the most generator-hostile setup (fast parallax, huge scale change, lens covered). The references keep the camera 5–30 m from the subject.

## Realism notes

- Good: film grain, warm twilight, dust that stays low and spreads, tail lights through sand, wheel blur.
- Weak: the car's rear is soft in clip 3 (480p draft look); the camels in clip 4 are doll-sized; the airborne car has no weight; the sand wall in clip 3 moves slower than the dust in 2 and 4, so the two storms do not feel like the same weather.
